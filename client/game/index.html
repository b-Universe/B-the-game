Come help make B!

B is aimed at being a collaborative, community-driven project.

We're actively looking for developers, writers, and especially artists who want to help shape this 16-bit digital world.

No Generative AI. We stand firmly with digital artists and developers. We don't use prompt-generation to create our art, lore, or code logic. We want only raw human creativity here, though we do embrace ethical, structural automation. Check out our AI & Automation Boundary for our exact stance.

Our current goal is building a playable prototype to showcase the game's potential. Once ready, we'll launch a fundraiser specifically to pay a dedicated pixel artist!
