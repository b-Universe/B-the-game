Melee powersets are designed for high-impact, close-quarters engagement. In **B**, your physical composition dictates not just the damage you deal, but the very nature of the weapon you wield. Access to these pools is governed by your position on the **Bio-Synthetic Scale**.

---

## Pure Melee [ 0% Integrity / 100% Purity ]

**Theme:** Martial mastery, ancestral relics, and elemental phase-shifting.

**Synergy:** These sets rely on the inherent **Efficiency** of the soul and body. They focus on **The Flow** - stacking minor buffs with every successful hit to unleash massive finishers.

* **`B Fu` (Martial Arts):** A high-mobility set focused on strikes, kicks, and nerve-strikes. Synergy relies on **Momentum**; higher attack speed increases your **Dodge** chance.
* **`Heritage Blades` (Swords/Spears):** Precise and balanced traditional weaponry. Synergy focuses on **Parry**, allowing players to negate incoming damage and immediately counter-attack.
* **`Phase-Shift Hydromancy`:** Manipulating water through extreme temperature shifts. Synergy allows players to cycle between **Steam** (High-temp/DoT) and **Freezing** (Low-temp/Control).
    * **`Steam Bursts`:** High-pressure geysers that launch enemies and apply heavy **Burning** damage.
    * **`Cryo-Strikes`:** Flash-freezing water upon impact to apply the **Hold** or **Slow** status effects.
* **`Arcane Affinity` (Fire / Lightning / Earth):** Elemental melee combat. Synergy allows for **Status Effects** like **Burn** or **Stun** to be applied directly through physical weapon contact.
* **`Heroic Might` (Super Strength):** Raw, unaugmented biological power. Synergy focuses on **Knockback**, allowing players to disrupt enemy formations through sheer force.

---

## Synthetic Melee [ -100% Synthetic Exclusive ]

**Theme:** High-energy physics, industrial force, and acoustic destruction.

**Synergy:** These sets operate on **Explicit Logic**. They deal consistent, maximum damage without variance, but build **System Latency** (Heat) that must be managed to avoid performance limitations.

* **`Vibratory Resonance` (Sonic):** Utilizing high-frequency sound emitters to shatter molecular bonds. Synergy focuses on **Internal Resonance**, where sonic vibrations bypass physical armor to deal direct damage.
    * **`Shatter Point`:** A precise strike that applies the **Fragile** status effect, increasing all incoming damage to the target for a short duration.
* **`Anti-Matter Striker`:** Stabilized anti-matter fields contained within heavy gauntlets. Synergy focuses on **Molecule Deconstruction**, where hits ignore a high percentage of enemy **Resistance**.
* **`Hydraulic Heavy` (Oversized Axes / Maces):** Massive, gear-assisted weapons. Synergy relies on **Inertia**; once an attack animation begins, it harder to interrupt with most **Status Effects**.
* **`Servo-Strength`:** Mechanical actuators providing artificial super-strength. Synergy allows for **Grappling** and **Crushing**, providing near perfect hit rates.

---

## Mutated & Ethereal Melee [ +100% Mutated Exclusive ]

**Theme:** Evolutionary weaponry, psychokinetic projections, and visceral fury.

**Synergy:** These sets rely on **Evolutionary Overcome**. They are volatile and gain significant power as the player takes damage or enters a **Feral Instinct** state.

* **`Psionic Bladestorm`:** Mutated **Neural** players projecting telekinetic swords that float around the character. Synergy relies on the **Collective Will**; the number of floating blades scales with the proximity of other **Neural** players in the group.
    * **`Ghost Striking`:** The blades attack independently of the player's primary animation, allowing for simultaneous defense and offense.
* **`Bone-Wrought Weapons` (Blades / Spikes):** Calcified weapons grown directly from the player's skeletal structure. Synergy focuses on **Bleed** and **`Laceration`**, dealing massive damage over time.
* **`Bio-Radiation Melee`:** The player's body acts as a leaking biological reactor. Synergy creates an **Irradiated Aura**, causing nearby enemies to lose **`Regeneration`** and suffer ticking damage.
* **`Apex Hypertrophy` (Super Strength):** Biological muscles grown to impossible, distorted sizes. Synergy relies on **Adrenaline Pulse**; damage output increases as the player's HP decreases.

---

## Hybrid Exclusives [ Discoverable ]

> *Note: These powersets require specific blends of Synthetic, Mutation, and Purity to unlock through advanced character development.*

* **`Quantum B Fu` (Synthetic + Pure):** Martial arts utilizing short-range telemetry-warps. Strikes occur from multiple angles simultaneously.
* **`Necrotic Engine` (Synthetic + Ethereal):** Reanimating mechanical parts with soul-energy to create self-repairing weapons.
* **`Unstable Singularity` (Mutation + Synthetic):** Forcing anti-matter into biological cells. High damage output, but creates a chance for the player to detonate upon death.

---

## Synergy Overview: The B Multiplier

Regardless of the chosen set, all melee players benefit from the account-wide **Efficiency** rating. In a **Neural** group, melee players act as the anchor for the group. 

* **The Engagement:** A player using **Vibratory Resonance** or **Psionic Bladestorm** draws enemy attention.
* **The Shred:** Player's **Sync** multipliers from the rest of the group increases the melee player’s **Accuracy** and **Critical** chance.
