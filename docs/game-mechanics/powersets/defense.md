Defense powersets prioritize **Avoidance** over mitigation. While **Resistance** focuses on hardening the body to survive a hit, **Defense** ensures the hit never connects in the first place. These sets rely heavily on high **Efficiency** and timing to maintain a "untouchable" status on the battlefield.

---

## Pure Defense [ 0% Integrity / 100% Purity ]

**Theme:** Martial mastery, spiritual foresight, and peak physical agility.

**Synergy:** These sets rely on **Reflexes** and **Intuition**. They reward players who maintain high **Momentum**, increasing the chance to dodge proportionally to their movement speed and successful combat flow.

* **B Fu (Defensive Arts):** A style focused on redirection and circular movement. Synergy allows the player to use an enemy's own momentum against them, triggering a **Dodge-Counter** that deals damage without taking any.
* **Elusive Footwork:** Mastery of positioning and smoke-like movement. Synergy focuses on **Displacement**, creating a "blur" effect that makes it difficult for enemies to lock on with ranged attacks.
* **Ancestral Cloak:** Utilizing traditional garments or charms imbued with spirit energy. Synergy provides high resistance to debuffs and **Status Effects**.

---

## Synthetic Defense [ -100% Synthetic Exclusive ]

**Theme:** High-speed telemetry, optical manipulation, and jet-assisted evasion.

**Synergy:** These sets operate on **Explicit Logic** and **Sensor Throughput**. They provide calculated avoidance but are vulnerable to **Signal Interference** and build **System Latency** if overused.

* **Telemetry Evasion:** Advanced sensor arrays that calculate the trajectory of incoming projectiles. Synergy allows for a **Perfect Dodge** against ranged attacks, provided the player has sufficient **Electricity** to power the processors.
* **Optical Camouflage:** Active light-bending technology that renders the player invisible to the naked eye. Synergy focuses on **Stealth**, making the player untargetable until they initiate an attack.
* **Jet-Assisted Evasion:** Utilizing micro-thrusters for sudden, high-speed dashes. Synergy allows the player to instantly exit **AOE (Area of Effect)** zones, effectively negating floor-based hazards.
* **Holographic Decoys:** Deploying high-fidelity hard-light illusions. Synergy creates temporary **Pets** that draw enemy fire, allowing the primary player to remain undetected.

---

## Mutation / Aether Defense [ +100% Mutated Exclusive ]

**Theme:** Reactive biology, phase-shifting, and feral instinct.

**Synergy:** These sets rely on **Evolutionary Overcome**. They are often reactive, triggered by the proximity of threats or the sudden spike of adrenaline in the player's system.

* **Aetheric Foresight:** Tapping into the **Ethereal** flow to see attacks seconds before they happen. Synergy provides a massive baseline **Defense** bonus that increases as the player’s **Energy Source** (Aether) remains high.
* **Feral Instinct:** A hyper-reactive nervous system that bypasses conscious thought. Synergy provides a massive **Reflex** bonus that triggers when the player's health is low.
* **Bioluminescent Blinding:** Emitting sudden, high-intensity flashes of light from specialized organs. Synergy reduces the **Accuracy** of all nearby enemies, effectively increasing the player’s defense through disruption.
* **Reactive Camouflage:** Skin that shifts color and texture to match the environment instantly. Synergy increases **Defense** while the player is stationary or moving slowly, perfect for ambushes.
* **Static Flicker (Plasma / Electric):** Utilizing electrical aether to "flicker" through space. Synergy provides a chance to **Blink** a short distance away whenever the player is about to be hit.

---

## Hybrid Exclusives

> Note: These powersets require specific blends of Synthetic, Mutation, and Purity to unlock through advanced character development.

* **Digital Lifeforce (Synthetic + Pure):** A hybrid of high-speed telemetry and spiritual displacement. Allows the player to exist in a state of "Quantum Uncertainty," where they are immune to being hit for as long as they are moving.
* **Vortex Shell (Synthetic + Mutation):** Utilizing gravitational sensors and elastic biology to create a field that bends projectiles around the player.
* **Living Shadow (Mutation + Ethereal):** Fusing biological darkness with spiritual energy. The player becomes a living shadow, making them immune to all non-elemental damage while in dim light.

---

## Synergy Overview: The B Multiplier

In a **Neural** group, Defense players serve as the "Ghost Fleet."

* **The Distraction:** By maintaining high **Evasion**, these players draw enemy fire and "waste" enemy turns, as attacks consistently fail to land.
* **Sync Factor:** The **Distributed Efficiency** of a group increases when Defense players are present, as the group takes less total damage, reducing the strain on **Resistance** anchors and healers.
