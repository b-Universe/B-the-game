# Powersets: Support Powersets

Support powersets are the backbone of any group. They focus exclusively on restoring health, amplifying damage output, and shielding allies. While they often lack individual offensive power, their impact is multiplicative within a **Neural** group or party.

---

## Pure Support [ 0% Integrity / 100% Purity ]

**Theme:** Natural optimization, battlefield leadership, and peak physical medicine.

**Synergy:** These sets rely on **Presence** and **Flow**. Buffs from these pools are often passive or persistent, radiating from the player to stabilize the team’s performance through biological excellence.

* **Fitness & Natural Generation:** Maintaining peak biological uptime for the group. Synergy focuses on passive health and energy recovery that scales with the group’s total **Purity**.
* **Inspirational Presence:** Utilizing raw charisma and leadership to bolster spirits. Synergy provides a flat bonus to **Accuracy** and **Critical** multipliers for the entire party.
* **Battle Medic:** Mastery of physical first aid and trauma surgery. Synergy involves high-speed manual healing and the ability to **Cleanse** status effects using traditional kits.
* **Tactical Coordination:** Guiding allies through vocal or spiritual cues. Synergy focuses on **Morale**, reducing the **Energy Source** consumption of everyone in proximity.

---

## Synthetic Support [ -100% Synthetic Exclusive ]

**Theme:** Automated nanites, tactical data streams, and hardware overclocking.

**Synergy:** These sets operate on **Explicit Logic** and **System Throughput**. They provide precision-targeted buffs but are susceptible to **Signal Interference** and cost significant **Electricity**.

* **Nanite Mending:** Deploying swarms of microscopic drones to stitch wounds or repair chassis. Synergy focuses on **Automated Triage**, where the nanites automatically seek out the ally with the lowest health.
* **Tactical Overlay:** Uplinking a shared HUD (Heads-Up Display) to all members of the group. Synergy provides a massive boost to **Tracking**, allowing the group to ignore enemy **Defense** or **Camouflage** bonuses.
* **Overclocking Protocols:** Forcing ally systems to run at unsafe speeds. Synergy increases **Attack Speed** and **Movement Speed** significantly, but builds **System Latency** (Heat) in the targets.
* **Kinetic Shielding:** Projecting a shared energy envelope. Synergy provides a temporary **Resistance** floor to the entire group, absorbing a set amount of damage before failing.

---

## Mutation / Aether Support [ +100% Mutated Exclusive ]

**Theme:** Flesh-weaving, mutagenic mists, and aetheric restoration.

**Synergy:** These sets rely on **Evolutionary Overcome** and the manipulation of **Ethereal** flows. They are often visceral and high-impact, though they may increase **Metabolic Hunger** in biological allies.

* **Flesh-Weaving:** Using biological filaments to instantly knit tissue and bone back together. Synergy provides the most powerful burst healing in the game but requires a direct **Bio-Mass** cost from the caster.
* **Aetheric Restoration (Water / Steam):** Manipulating moisture to refresh the body and mind. Creates high-pressure "Mending Geysers" that heal everyone in an area while pushing enemies back.
* **Mutagenic Spores:** Emitting a cloud of beneficial bacteria. Synergy provides a temporary **Mutation** bonus to allies, increasing their **Regeneration** and physical damage output.
* **Neural Link:** Establishing a direct psychic bridge between allies. Synergy allows the Support player to take a portion of the damage dealt to an ally, while sharing their own **Energy Source** pool with the group.

---

## Hybrid Exclusives

> Note: These powersets require specific blends of Synthetic, Mutation, and Purity to unlock through advanced character development.

* **System-Sync (Synthetic + Pure):** Fusing tactical software with peak human intuition. Allies gain **Predictive Guard**, granting them a better reflexes to dodge attacks.
* **Bio-Electronic Stim (Synthetic + Mutation):** Using synthetic nodes to trigger evolutionary adrenaline spikes. Provides a massive, temporary boost to all stats with zero **System Latency**.
* **Ethereal Battery (Ethereal + Synthetic/Mutation):** Converting spiritual **Ethereal** energy into a usable charge for others. Acts as a mobile recharging station for allies using aetheric powersets.

---

## Synergy Overview: The B Multiplier

In a **Neural** group, Support players act as the Signal Booster.

* **The Battery:** By managing the health and energy of the group, Support players allow **Resistance** anchors to soak more damage and **Ranged** players to fire indefinitely.
* **Sync Factor:** The effectiveness of Support buffs scales with **Distributed Efficiency**.
