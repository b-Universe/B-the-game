Resistance powersets focus on damage mitigation, survival throughput, and the ability to soak incoming fire. Unlike **Defense**, which focuses on avoidance, **Resistance** ensures that the damage that does land is minimized through physical or energetic hardening.

---

## Pure Resistance [ 0% Integrity / 100% Purity ]

**Theme:** Mental discipline, ancestral relics, and peak biological conditioning.

**Synergy:** These sets rely on **Willpower** and **Flow**. They focus on internal resolve and physical mastery to negate the impact of incoming strikes.

* **Martial Reflexes:** Utilizing advanced combat training to roll with punches and deflect strikes. Synergy focuses on **Positional Resistance**, where damage taken is reduced based on facing the enemy directly.
* **Willpower:** Pure mental defiance against physical pain. Synergy provides high resistance to **Status Effects** and allows the player to remain standing even when HP is critically low.
* **Physical Armor & Shields:** Mastery over traditional protection. Synergy focuses on **Deflection**, providing a flat damage reduction against kinetic and piercing attacks.
* **Field Gadgets:** Using non-synthetic tools and manual equipment for survival. Synergy involves deployable physical cover and manual medical kits that provide a stable baseline of protection.

---

## Synthetic Resistance [ -100% Synthetic Exclusive ]

**Theme:** High-energy physics, industrial plating, and tactical logic.

**Synergy:** These sets operate on **Explicit Logic** and **Throughput**. They provide massive, reliable mitigation but build **System Latency** (Heat) and are vulnerable to **EMP** interference.

* **Invulnerability:** Full-body industrial reinforcement utilizing titanium or carbon-fiber alloys. Synergy focuses on **Damage Thresholds**, where attacks below a certain power level deal zero damage.
* **Force Field Projection:** Deploying high-wattage energy barriers around the player. Synergy allows the shield to absorb 100% of damage until its **Battery** is depleted, requiring a recharge cycle.
* **Tactical Gadgets:** Integrated hardware systems for survival. Synergy involves automated nanite-repair drones and flash-shunts that momentarily blind attackers to reduce their output.
* **Kinetic Manipulation:** Actuators that absorb and redirect shock. Synergy converts a percentage of incoming damage into **Energy Source** (Electricity) for the player's offensive powers.

---

## Mutation / Aether Resistance [ +100% Mutated Exclusive ]

**Theme:** Reactive evolution, elemental aether manipulation, and visceral recovery.

**Synergy:** These sets rely on **Evolutionary Overcome**. They are reactive, growing stronger and more durable the longer a fight lasts or as the player utilizes **Ethereal** sources to manifest **Aether** defenses.

* **Hyper-Regeneration:** Cellular repair on a localized overdrive setting. Synergy focuses on **Recovery Spikes**, where taking a massive hit triggers a temporary massive increase in healing speed.
* **Chitinous Exoskeleton:** Growing a hardened, insectoid shell over the flesh. Synergy provides a stacking **Armor** bonus every time the player is hit, up to a massive cap.
* **Bio-Electric Discharge:** Utilizing internal organs for repelling fields. Synergy shocks any enemy in melee contact, dealing damage back and reducing their attack speed.
* **Parasitic Absorption:** Microscopic organisms that consume incoming energy. Synergy converts a portion of incoming damage into **Bio-Mass**, helping to stave off **Metabolic Hunger**.
* **Elasticity:** Mutating the body to be semi-liquid or rubberized. Synergy allows the player to absorb **Blunt Trauma** and **Knockback**.
* **Aetheric Aquatic Cycling:** Manipulating moisture via aetheric temperature shifts. Creates a frost-glaze for armor or a steam-shroud to dampen kinetic force.
* **Aetheric Grounding (Fire / Electric / Plasma):** Channeling hostile elemental aether through the player's architecture. Synergy provides near-immunity to **Burn**, **Short-Circuit**, and **Meltdown** status effects.

---

## Hybrid Exclusives

> Note: These powersets require specific blends of Synthetic, Mutation, and Purity to unlock through advanced character development.

* **Radiation & Anti-Matter (Synthetic + Mutation):** Utilizing lead-lined biological growths to contain an anti-matter core to develop complex resistance and defenses.
* **Neural Plate Armor (Synthetic + Pure):** A hybrid of logic-driven hardware and martial willpower. Provides a perfect balance between **Damage Thresholds** and **Status Effect** immunity.
* **Ghost-Circuitry (Synthetic + Ethereal):** Integrating spiritual **Ethereal** energy into synthetic hardware. Allows the player to partially phase out of reality, making them resistant to all physical damage for short bursts.

---

## Synergy Overview: The "B" Multiplier

In a **Neural** cluster, Resistance players serve as the primary anchor for **Distributed Trauma**.

* **The Soak:** By absorbing damage intended for the player and distributing it among the group, the Resistance player ensures the **Distributed Efficiency** establishes a health pool of it's own.
* **Sync Factor:** The more players within proximity of their group increase the strength of resistances exponentially.
