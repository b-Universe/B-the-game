Ranged powersets are the primary method for dealing high-volume damage from a position of safety. In **B**, these sets rely on the user's **Efficiency** to maintain distance and accuracy. Access to specific projectile types and hardware is dictated by your **Integrity Axis** composition.

---

## Pure Ranged [ 0% Integrity / 100% Purity ]

**Synergy:** These sets reward high **Dopamine** and manual timing. They focus on **Critical Distance** - landing shots from specific ranges to maximize the impact of the **Purity** bonus.

* **Archery (Bow & Crossbow):** Utilizing traditional strings and bolts empowered by the user's essence. Synergy focuses on **Bleed** and **Piercing**, allowing shots to pass through multiple enemies in a line. Bows are faster but weaker, Crossbows are more accurate, faster, and stronger, but slower.
* **Slingshot:** A gritty, high-impact set for the resourceful individualist. Synergy relies on **Payload Variance**, where players can swap between high-stun lead pellets and high-flame resin balls.
* **Tactical Ballistics (Assault Rifle / Dual Pistols):** High-velocity lead delivery. Synergy focuses on **Suppressive Fire**, where continuous shooting applies a movement and accuracy debuff to enemies in the target area.
* **Elemental Flux (Arcane Lightning / Fire):** Projecting raw aetheric energy through the hands. Synergy allows for **Chain Reaction**, where lightning arcs between enemies and fire creates persistent **Burn** patches on the ground.
* **Hydro-Ballistics (Water / Ice):** Projecting pressurized streams or jagged ice shards. Synergy focuses on **Thermal Weight**, alternating between heavy water pressure that knocks enemies back and flash-freezing ice that applies the **Hold** status.
* **Olympic Ballistics(Super Strength):** Utilizing peak biological force to launch heavy spears, shotputs, and throw ballistic objects. Synergy focuses on **Impaling** and **Knockback**, pinning and forcing enemies to walls or the ground, effectively locking or slowing their movement.

---

## Synthetic Ranged [ -100% Synthetic Exclusive ]

**Synergy:** These sets operate on **Explicit Logic** and high **System Throughput**. They provide stability but are vulnerable to **EMP** lockouts and build **System Latency** (Heat).

* **Photon Lance (Laser Beam):** A continuous, high-intensity energy stream. Synergy relies on **Thermal Build-up**; the longer the beam is held on a single target, the higher the damage becomes until the system requires a vent cycle.
* **Antimatter Pulse:** Launching stabilized pockets of antimatter. Synergy focuses on **Molecular Decay**, where hits disintegrate enemy armor, permanently lowering **Resistance** for the duration of the encounter.
* **Energy Array (Radioactive Energy):** High-energy plasma bolts launched in rapid succession. Synergy creates **Fallout Zones** at the point of impact, dealing continuous damage to any entity standing in the radioactive residue.

---

## Mutated Ranged [ +100% Mutated Exclusive ]

**Theme:** Biological projectile systems, psionic shrapnel, and evolutionary force.

**Synergy:** These sets rely on **Evolutionary Overcome**. They are often Self-Sourced, meaning the player consumes their own **Bio-Mass** or **Metabolic Hunger** to generate ammunition.

* **Quill Barrage (Biological Darts):** Launching rapid-fire, calcified bone spines from the forearms. Synergy focuses on **Toxic Coating**, applying stacks of **Poison** that slow the target's attack speed.
* **Earthen Catapult (Projectile Earth):** Ripping chunks of the environment from the ground via super-strength and launching them. Synergy focuses on **Blunt Trauma**, dealing massive damage and **Stunning** enemies.
* **Psychokinetic Volley:** **Neural** players projecting mental force into loose objects or psionic shards. Synergy relies on **Shared Sight**; if any neural or node in the group sees a target, the group can track it around corners.
* **Bio-Acidic Glands:** Spitting or launching high-morbidity corrosive fluids. Synergy focuses on **Toxic**, where the acid eats through the target's defense and deals high damage over time to biological and mechanical targets alike.

---

## Hybrid Exclusives [ Discoverable ]

> *Note: These powersets require specific blends of Synthetic, Mutation, and Purity to unlock through advanced character development.*

* **Storm-Trigger (Synthetic + Lightning):** Integrating a lightning-affinity soul into a high-speed railgun. Projects Electric Slugs that stun and detonate on impact.
* **Void-Shot (Pure + Antimatter):** Aether-stabilized antimatter. Bypasses all physical shielding and deals direct Soul-Shock damage to the enemy's core.
* **Parasitic Spore-Cannon (Mutation + Industrial):** A mechanical launcher that fires biological spore-pods. The pods hatch upon impact, releasing temporary **Pets** that attack the target.

---

## Synergy Overview: The B Multiplier

Ranged players in a **Neural** cluster are the primary Shredders. While the melee players anchor the targets, the ranged players capitalize on the **Sync** multiplier.

* **The Volley:** In a group of players, the multiplicative **Accuracy** and **Critical** bonuses exponentially increase.
