Neural and Minion powersets focus on the command and control of multiple autonomous or semi-autonomous entities. Unlike the **Thralls** found in Control sets, these entities often function as specialized members of your **Group**, capable of following complex scripts, managing resources, and providing sustained presence in the world even when you are AFK.

---

## Pure Neural [ 0% Integrity / 100% Purity ]

**Theme:** Leadership, social influence, and mercenary contracts.

**Synergy:** These sets rely on **Influence** and **Wealth**. Instead of manifesting creatures from aether or building them from scrap, the player utilizes their status within the world to recruit living NPCs.

* **Vanguard Recruitment:** Convincing seasoned warriors and specialists to join your roster through shared experience and reputation. Synergy increases the **Loyalty** of NPCs, reducing the cost of their maintenance.
* **Mercenary Contracts:** Hiring professional muscle using wealth. Synergy focuses on **Contract Efficiency**, allowing the player to manage larger groups of hired NPCs for lower recurring costs.
* **Diplomatic Retinue:** Gathering a following of non-combat specialists (Healers, Crafters, Scouts). Synergy provides passive bonuses to **Resource Acquisition** and group-wide **Stamina** regeneration.

---

## Synthetic Neural [ -100% Synthetic Exclusive ]

**Theme:** Automated fabrication, drone networking, and robotic warfare.

**Synergy:** These sets operate on **Explicit Logic** and **Networking**. The player acts as a mobile command center, deploying hardware that follows strict sub-routines. These units are immune to psychological status effects but vulnerable to **EMP**.

* **Automated Robotics:** Deploying humanoid or quadrupedal combat bots. Synergy focuses on **Array Tactics**, where the robots share sensor data to increase their collective **Accuracy**.
* **Drone Swarm:** Releasing a cloud of small, high-speed aerial drones. Synergy allows the drones to act as a mobile **Tactical Overlay**, providing 360-degree vision and highlighting enemy weaknesses.
* **Sentry Turrets:** Deploying stationary or slow-moving defensive emplacements. Synergy creates **Kill Zones**, where the turrets focus-fire on the same target to exponentially increase damage.

---

## Mutation / Aether Neural [ +100% Mutated Exclusive ]

**Theme:** Necrotic reanimation, elemental critters, and biological hiveminds.

**Synergy:** These sets rely on **Evolutionary Overcome** and **Ethereal** channeling. They involve the reanimation of biological matter or the summoning of minor aetheric spirits that serve the host.

* **Necrotic Reanimation:** Fusing biological mutation with **Ethereal** energy to raise fallen NPCs as zombies or husks. Synergy focuses on **Horde Mentality**, where the strength and resistance of the undead increase based on the size of the pack.
* **Aetheric Familiars:** Manifesting small, elemental critters (Fire Sprites, Ice Wisps, Electric Motes). Synergy allows these familiars to act as living batteries, restoring the player's **Energy Source** when they dissipate.
* **Parasitic Brood:** Using the player’s own body as a carrier for biological minions. Synergy requires constant **Bio-Mass** to sustain the brood, but provides highly aggressive, self-replicating units that overwhelm enemies through numbers.

---

## Hybrid Exclusives

> Note: These powersets require specific blends of Synthetic, Mutation, and Purity to unlock through advanced character development.

* **Scrap-Stitched Golems (Synthetic + Ethereal):** Utilizing necrotic energy to possess heaps of discarded industrial scrap. These minions are highly durable and can self-repair using fallen mechanical parts.
* **Bio-Mechanical Symbiotes (Synthetic + Mutation):** Grafting synthetic processors onto mutated biological organisms to create Cyborg Minions with high regeneration and logic-driven accuracy.
* **Soul-Bound Constructs (Pure + Ethereal):** Binding a friendly spirit to a physical vessel or armor set. These minions have high **Ethereal** resistance and can bypass physical armor.

---

## Synergy Overview: The B Multiplier

In a **Neural** group, Minion-heavy players act as the Frontline Buffer.

* **The Numbers Game:** By flooding the field with entities, the player dilutes enemy targeting, ensuring that high-value players take less direct focus.
* **Sync Factor:** The **Distributed Trauma** skill is exceptionally powerful here; damage dealt to the primary player can be shunted off to their minions, significantly increasing the player's effective health pool.
