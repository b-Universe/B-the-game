# Icon Symbology

To maintain intuitive clarity across the **B** archive, powerset icons use a standardized visual language inspired by the **Power Icons Legend**. This system ensures every operator can identify targeting logic, damage types, and status effects at a glance without sacrificing the gritty 90s indie comic aesthetic.

## Icon Color Theory

Powerset icons are color-coded based on their position on the **Integrity Axis**. This visual shorthand allows operators to identify the origin and nature of a manifest's power at a glance.

| Category | Primary Color | Examples | Association |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Primal / Arcane** | Indigo / Violet | <i class="b-icon primal affects-target-only extreme-ranged"></i><i class="b-icon primal location teleport-caster"></i><i class="b-icon primal location summon-undead"></i> | Ethereal stability and ancient heritage. |
| **Human / Baseline** | White | <i class="b-icon human affects-target-only light-minor-ranged"></i><i class="b-icon human run-speed-and-jump-height"></i><i class="b-icon human affects-target-only light-minor-melee"></i> | Standard adaptability and soul architecture. |
| **Mutation** | Acid Green | <i class="b-icon mutation cone moderate-ranged"></i><i class="b-icon mutation heal"></i><i class="b-icon mutation recovery"></i> | Biological instability and viral pathogens. |
| **Synthetic** | Neon Cyan | <i class="b-icon synthetic summon summon-minor"></i><i class="b-icon synthetic pbaoe slow"></i><i class="b-icon synthetic pbaoe-allies resistance-defense-1"></i> | Hardware integration and network efficiency. |
| **Robotic** | Gold | <i class="b-icon robotic targeted-aoe snipe"></i><i class="b-icon robotic large-defense-bonus"></i><i class="b-icon robotic summon summon-shitload"></i> | Absolute technological replacement (Automaton). |

Visual clarity is only the first step in manifest management. To understand how these symbols interact with actual gameplay mechanics and the narrative stability of **B**, consult the following records:

* **The Integrity Axis**: A deep-dive into the shifting spectrum between Primal and Robotic origins and how color-mix logic dictates manifest stability.
* **Archetypes - Powersets**: A comprehensive catalog of how specific engagement roles utilize these symbology stacks to define their combat signatures.

Detailed documentation on these systems can be found within the [Beginner's Guide](#/account/beginners-guide) in the character creation.
